Collaboration

Conversations and shared work with colleagues in healthcare simulation, adult learning, and higher education.

I welcome conversations about healthcare simulation, adult learning, higher education, leadership, and professional development. If you're interested in collaborating on research, presentations, educational initiatives, or scholarly work, I'd be glad to hear from you.

Much of what I've learned has come from conversations with colleagues who were working through similar questions. Whether you're a fellow student, an educator, a simulation professional, or a researcher, I'm always happy to connect, share ideas, and learn from one another.
